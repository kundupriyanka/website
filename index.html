I’m Priyanka Kundu, a doctoral candidate in Communication at the University of Illinois Chicago.

I study how media and emerging technologies redistribute communicative power—among political elites, media institutions, and ordinary users—across Global South and transnational contexts.

01 / Research

Power in an evolving media environment.

My research connects digital political communication, journalism, platform affordances, and generative AI. I ask how stories become visible, whose perspectives they represent, and how technologies shape public life.

I use qualitative, quantitative, computational, and mixed methods, with a particular emphasis on critical discourse analysis, interviews, and the study of platforms.

I / POLITICAL ELITES

Platforms & political narratives

How political actors use platform features to construct narratives and exercise discursive power.

II / MEDIA & AI INSTITUTIONS

Technology & representation

How journalism and AI shape public understandings of technology, cultural representation, and accountability.

III / PARTICIPATING PUBLICS

Remix & public participation

How ordinary users appropriate and contest political meanings through participatory culture.

Dissertation

Platformed Election Campaigning

My dissertation examines Facebook affordances and political narratives in Bangladesh’s 2024 election. It compares official party pages to understand how platform features and political context shape campaign storytelling.

Walkthrough method · Post coding · Critical discourse analysis

AI research

What is Radical?

Developed during my 2026 DDC × TrygFonden Fellowship at the University of Southern Denmark, this project probes how generative AI defines a contested political concept through stories in Bangla and English.

Generative AI · Framing · Language & transnational comparison
