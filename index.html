I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Communication at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC). My research examines the intersections of digital media, political communication, platform power, and emerging technologies, with particular attention to the Global South and transnational contexts.

I investigate how digital platforms shape political narratives, public discourse, and collective identities. My current work also explores the social and political implications of artificial intelligence, including generative AI, automated journalism, and the changing information environment.

About

My academic background combines journalism, media studies, and communication research. Before joining UIC, I worked as a lecturer in the Department of Mass Communication and Journalism at Bangladesh University of Professionals and as a journalist with United News of Bangladesh.

These experiences inform my interest in the relationship between media institutions, political power, and public communication, particularly in societies experiencing democratic uncertainty and technological transformation. I approach communication as both a cultural practice and a site where political narratives, identities, and inequalities are produced and contested.
