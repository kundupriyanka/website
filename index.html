Communication Scholar

Priyanka Kundu

Doctoral Candidate (ABD), Department of Communication
University of Illinois Chicago

I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Communication at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC). My research examines the intersections of digital media, political communication, platform power, and emerging technologies, with particular attention to the Global South and transnational contexts.

My work investigates how digital platforms shape political narratives, public discourse, and collective identities. I am also interested in the social and political implications of artificial intelligence, including the ways generative AI systems influence information environments, representation, and democratic communication.

My dissertation examines platformed election campaigning through an analysis of Facebook affordances and political narratives during Bangladesh's 2024 election.
