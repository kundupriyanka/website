Welcome !

I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Communication at the University of Illinois Chicago. My research examines how digital platforms and emerging technologies shape political communication, public discourse, and information environments, with particular attention to Bangladesh and the Global South.

My dissertation, Platformed Election Campaigning: Facebook Affordances and Political Narratives in Bangladesh’s 2024 Election, investigates the relationship between platform features and political narratives during an important electoral moment.

My broader interests include platform governance, computational communication research, artificial intelligence and journalism, political framing, and digital democracy.
