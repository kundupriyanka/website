Welcome!

I am a communication scholar specializing in political communication, emerging media, and cultural studies. I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Communication at the University of Illinois Chicago and a 2026 DDC × TrygFonden Fellow at the University of Southern Denmark.

My research examines the intersection of political communication, cultural studies, and journalism in digital media and AI. I am a mixed-methods researcher with particular strengths in qualitative methods, including discourse analysis, reflexive thematic analysis, digital ethnography, the walkthrough method, and in-depth interviewing. I also apply framing analysis, content analysis, and small-scale computational text analysis in my research.
